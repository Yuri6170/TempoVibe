How this page works

The same engine as the iOS app

Every loop is a MIDI file, not audio — so retiming it means editing three bytes in a tempo event, not stretching a waveform. This page does that rewrite in JavaScript, in your browser, the moment you press play: fetch the loop, patch its tempo to your target BPM, hand it to a General MIDI synth, done.

Loop selection

Landing exactly on your target BPM first; failing that, the nearest tempo within ±20 BPM; failing that, half-time or double-time of what you asked for. The same fallback order as the app.

Metronome

Not a separate click track running on its own clock — it's baked in as one more MIDI track, on the exact same tick timeline as the drums, using real General MIDI percussion notes (wood block, triangle). Same synth, same clock, so it can't drift out of time.
